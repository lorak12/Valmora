---
title: "Tutorial: your first mob"
summary: Create a levelled custom mob with equipment and a loot table, spawn it, and make it appear naturally in the world.
---

You'll make a **Frost Wraith**: a level 10 skeleton with its own health, damage and defense that
can drop the Frostbrand sword from [the first tutorial](/docs/tutorial-first-item). About ten
minutes.

## Step 1 — Define the mob

Mobs live in `plugins/Valmora/mobs/`. Create `plugins/Valmora/mobs/my_mobs.yml`:

```yaml
# plugins/Valmora/mobs/my_mobs.yml
frost_wraith:
  name: "<aqua>Frost Wraith"
  type: STRAY
  category: UNDEAD
  level: 10
  stats:
    health: 400
    damage: 40
    defense: 50
    speed: 0.3
```

- `frost_wraith:` — the mob's id.
- `type` — the vanilla entity it's built on ([EntityType](https://jd.papermc.io/paper/1.21.11/org/bukkit/entity/EntityType.html) name). Its look and AI come from this.
- `category` — **required**. A group used by quests (`kill UNDEAD`) and skills. Built in: `UNDEAD`,
  `ENDER`, `NETHER`, `BEAST`, `AQUATIC`, `ARTHROPOD`, `ILLAGER`, `GOLEM`, `BOSS`, `OTHER`.
- `level` — shown on the nameplate, adds `level − 1` to its damage, and multiplies the combat XP it
  gives.
- `stats` — Valmora health, damage and defense (see [Mobs](/docs/mobs#stats)). Mob stat keys are
  lower case with hyphens: `crit-chance`, not `crit_chance`.

## Step 2 — Spawn it

```text
/mob reload
/mob spawn frost_wraith
```

A stray appears with a nameplate like `[Lv.10] Frost Wraith 400/400❤`, which updates as you hit
it. `/mob info` while looking at it shows its definition.

<Callout type="warning" title="Mob didn't load?">
The console shows `Failed to load some mobs` with the reason, e.g.
`In mob 'frost_wraith': Missing required field 'category'`. Fix it and `/mob reload`.
</Callout>

## Step 3 — Equipment and rewards

```yaml
# plugins/Valmora/mobs/my_mobs.yml
frost_wraith:
  name: "<aqua>Frost Wraith"
  type: STRAY
  category: UNDEAD
  level: 10
  base-xp: 5
  gold-reward: 25
  stats:
    health: 400
    damage: 40
    defense: 50
    speed: 0.3
  equipment:
    main-hand: BOW
    helmet: ICE                 # any material or Valmora item id
  loot-table:
    drops:
      - item: ICE
        min-amount: 1
        max-amount: 4
        chance: 0.8
      - item: frostbrand
        chance: 0.02
        luck-affected: true
```

<Callout type="warning">
Every item you name must exist. An unknown item id in `loot-table` makes **the whole mob** fail
to load, so if you skipped the first tutorial, remove the `frostbrand` drop.
</Callout>

- `base-xp: 5` — the killer gets `base-xp × level` = 50 Combat XP.
- `gold-reward: 25` — 25 coins go straight into the killer's purse.
- `equipment` — `main-hand`, `off-hand`, `helmet`, `chestplate`, `leggings`, `boots`. Vanilla
  materials or Valmora item ids.
- `loot-table.drops` — each entry rolls on its own. `chance` goes from `0` to `1` (`0.02` = 2%).
  `luck-affected: true` multiplies the chance by `1 + Luck / 100`, so a player with 100 Luck gets
  4%.

Reload, spawn it, kill it. The drops go into your inventory, and anything that doesn't fit drops
at your feet.

## Step 4 — Let it spawn on its own

Mobs don't spawn by themselves unless you ask. The simplest way is a `natural-spawn` block:

```yaml
  natural-spawn:
    enabled: true
    chance: 0.05       # 5% chance per check, per online player
    max-nearby: 3      # no more than 3 within 32 blocks of that player
```

Every 10 seconds, for each online player, Valmora rolls `chance` and spawns the mob on the surface
8–24 blocks away. That happens **everywhere**, in every world. To limit a mob to one area, use a
zone spawner instead — see [Tutorial: your first zone](/docs/tutorial-first-zone).

<Callout type="tip" title="Replacing vanilla mobs">
`natural-spawn.vanilla-default: true` turns **every** naturally spawned entity of that `type` into
this mob. Put it on a `ZOMBIE`-based definition and all zombies get your stats, name and loot.
Only one mob per entity type can have it.
</Callout>
