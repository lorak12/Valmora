---
title: "Tutorial: a boss fight"
summary: A boss with a health bar, timed and targeted abilities, an enrage at half health, and a second phase — all in YAML.
---

You'll build the **Stone Warden**: an iron-golem boss with a boss bar, a ground slam, a targeted
rock throw, an enrage that summons minions at 50% health, and a faster slam that replaces the first
one in phase two. Do [your first mob](/docs/tutorial-first-mob) first; this tutorial reuses its
Frost Wraith as the minion.

A boss is a normal mob with **abilities** and/or a **boss bar**; there's no separate boss system.

## Step 1 — The base mob

```yaml
# plugins/Valmora/mobs/my_boss.yml
stone_warden:
  name: "<red><bold>Stone Warden"
  type: IRON_GOLEM
  category: BOSS
  level: 30
  base-xp: 50
  gold-reward: 500
  persistent: true
  knockback-resistance: 1.0
  stats:
    health: 8000
    damage: 120
    defense: 150
    speed: 0.25
  boss-bar:
    enabled: true
    color: RED
    style: NOTCHED_10
    range: 40
```

- `persistent: true` — keeps it from despawning when players walk away. Its health and cooldowns
  are still lost on a restart.
- `knockback-resistance: 1.0` — can't be knocked back (0–1).
- `boss-bar` — shown to players within `range` blocks. `color`: `PINK`, `BLUE`, `RED`, `GREEN`,
  `YELLOW`, `PURPLE`, `WHITE`. `style`: `PROGRESS`, `NOTCHED_6`, `NOTCHED_10`, `NOTCHED_12`,
  `NOTCHED_20`.

`/mob reload`, then `/mob spawn stone_warden`. A red bar appears at the top of the screen.

## Step 2 — A timed area attack

Add an `abilities:` block. This slam fires every 7 seconds and hits every player within 6 blocks:

```yaml
  abilities:
    ground_slam:
      name: "Ground Slam"
      trigger: ON_TIMER
      interval: 140          # ticks (20 = 1 second)
      announce: "<red>The Stone Warden slams the ground!"
      mechanics:
        - type: DAMAGE
          params:
            damage: 150
            damage-type: MELEE
            target: "@enemies_in_radius{r=6}"
        - type: APPLY_EFFECT
          params:
            effect: slowness
            amplifier: 2
            duration: 3
            target: "@enemies_in_radius{r=6}"
```

- `trigger: ON_TIMER` — fires every `interval` ticks while the boss is alive. Add `chance: 0.5`
  to make each attempt a coin flip, or `cooldown: 10` (seconds) as a minimum gap.
- `announce` — sent to players within 40 blocks when the ability fires.
- Abilities use the **same mechanics as items** ([full list](/docs/abilities#mechanics)). For a
  mob, `@enemies_in_radius` means *players* near the boss.

<Callout type="warning" title="Messages from a boss ability">
Inside a boss ability, the "player" running the ability is the boss itself. Events that act on the
player running them — `notify`, `give`, `tag`, `variable` — do nothing there. Use `announce` for
messages, and `foreach @nearby:<radius> <event>` to run an event for every nearby player.
</Callout>

## Step 3 — A targeted attack

`@target` needs something to aim at. Give the ability a `target-range` and the boss picks the
nearest player within that many blocks:

```yaml
    rock_throw:
      name: "Rock Throw"
      trigger: ON_TIMER
      interval: 100
      target-range: 16
      announce: "<gray>The Stone Warden hurls a boulder!"
      mechanics:
        - type: DAMAGE
          params:
            damage: 90
            target: "@target"
        - type: APPLY_EFFECT
          params:
            effect: levitation
            amplifier: 1
            duration: 1.5
            target: "@target"
```

Without `target-range`, `@target` is empty and the ability hits nothing.

## Step 4 — Enrage at half health

`ON_HEALTH` fires **once**, the first time the boss drops to `health-percent` or below:

```yaml
    enrage:
      name: "Enrage"
      trigger: ON_HEALTH
      health-percent: 50
      announce: "<dark_red><bold>The Stone Warden enrages and calls for help!"
      mechanics:
        - type: SCRIPT
          params:
            events:
              - "spawn_mob frost_wraith 3 radius:5"
        - type: APPLY_EFFECT
          params:
            effect: strength
            amplifier: 2
            duration: -1
            target: "@self"
```

`SCRIPT` runs any [script event](/docs/events). Here `spawn_mob` summons three Frost Wraiths around
the boss, and `@self` (the boss) gets permanent Strength II.

## Step 5 — A second phase

Add a faster slam for phase two:

```yaml
    enraged_slam:
      name: "Enraged Slam"
      trigger: ON_TIMER
      interval: 60
      announce: "<dark_red>The ground shatters!"
      mechanics:
        - type: DAMAGE
          params:
            damage: 220
            damage-type: MELEE
            target: "@enemies_in_radius{r=8}"
```

Now both slams fire all the time. To make `ground_slam` phase-one-only and `enraged_slam`
phase-two-only, use a **pipeline**: a server-wide rule that runs just before any boss ability
fires. Open `plugins/Valmora/mob_pipeline.yml`, and replace its last line, `stages: []`, with:

```yaml
# plugins/Valmora/mob_pipeline.yml
stages:
  - id: warden_phase_one_only
    when: pre_ability
    conditions:
      - "$mob.id$ == stone_warden"
      - "$mob.ability_id$ == ground_slam"
      - "$mob.health_percent$ < 50"
    on-pass:
      - "interrupt"

  - id: warden_phase_two_only
    when: pre_ability
    conditions:
      - "$mob.id$ == stone_warden"
      - "$mob.ability_id$ == enraged_slam"
      - "$mob.health_percent$ >= 50"
    on-pass:
      - "interrupt"
```

Each stage runs before the boss fires an ability. When **all** of its `conditions` are true,
`on-pass` runs, and `interrupt` cancels that firing. `$mob.id$`, `$mob.ability_id$` and
`$mob.health_percent$` describe the boss and the ability it's about to use. See
[Pipelines](/docs/pipelines) for everything else a pipeline can hook.

<Callout type="tip">
Use underscores in ability ids you compare in conditions (`ground_slam`). In an expression,
`ground-slam` would be read as "ground minus slam".
</Callout>

## Step 6 — Test it

```text
/valmora reload
/mob spawn stone_warden
```

- The boss bar tracks its health; slams hit you when you're close and the rock throw hits you from
  range.
- At half health you see the enrage message, three Frost Wraiths appear, and the slow slam is
  replaced by the fast one.
- `/valmora pipeline list mob:pre_ability` should list both stages. If it doesn't, check the
  console for a `[PipelineYamlLoader]` warning.
