Valmora replaces vanilla damage with a stat-based formula. Health becomes a large pool (100 by
default, not 20 half-hearts) shown on a scaled 10-heart bar, and every hit shows a floating damage
number colored by its damage type.

## The damage formula

```text
damage = Damage × (1 + Strength / 100)
if the hit is critical (Crit Chance %): × (1 + Crit Damage / 100)
× enchant multipliers  × pipeline multipliers
× 100 / (Defense + 100)            victim's defense — skipped for types that ignore defense
− damage reduction, × (1 − victim's resistance to the damage type)
rounded down
```

- **Players** use their stats (Damage 5, Crit Chance 30%, Crit Damage 50% by default) plus
  weapon, armor and bonuses.
- **Custom mobs** use their `stats:` and level (see [Mobs](/docs/mobs#stats)). Plain vanilla mobs
  hit for `combat.fallback-base-damage`.
- 100 Defense halves damage, 300 cuts it to a quarter.
- Spam-clicking deals less: the vanilla attack-charge is applied (down to 20% at no charge,
  `combat.attack-cooldown`).
- Shields block 90% of a frontal hit; axes disable them (`combat.shield`).

## Environmental damage

Falling, fire, lava, drowning, explosions, poison and the like are multiplied by
`combat.environment-damage-multiplier` (5 by default) so they still matter against large health
pools, then mitigated by defense unless their damage type ignores it.

## Health, mana and regeneration

- Health regenerates `health_regen` per second, **except** within `combat.combat-window-ms` (3
  seconds) of dealing or taking damage.
- Mana regenerates `mana_regen` per second, always.
- Vanilla food regeneration is turned off for players; healing comes from stats, abilities and
  events.

All of these are in [config.yml → combat](/docs/config-yml#combat).

## Damage types

Every hit has a damage type. It sets the color of the damage number, whether defense applies,
and which mob `resistances:` reduce it.

<RefTable kind="damageTypes" />

They're defined in `plugins/Valmora/damage_types/*.yml`. You can add your own:

```yaml
# plugins/Valmora/damage_types/custom.yml
frost:
  color: "<aqua>"
  ignores-defense: false
  on-hit:
    - "apply_potion slowness 1 2 @target"
```

| Field | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `color` | `<white>` | Color of the damage number. |
| `ignores-defense` | `false` | Skip the victim's defense for this type. |
| `on-hit` | none | [Events](/docs/events) run every time a hit of this type lands. |

Use a type in `damage-type:` on the `DAMAGE` mechanic or a mob, in the `damage` event, and in mob
`resistances:` (`FROST: 0.5` = half damage, `1.0` = immune). Map vanilla damage causes to your
types with `combat.cause-mapping` in config.yml.

## Server-wide combat rules

To change damage in situations — low-health bonuses, zone rules, executes — use
`combat_pipeline.yml`. See [Pipelines](/docs/pipelines).
