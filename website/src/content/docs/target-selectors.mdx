---
title: Target selectors
summary: The @ selectors that choose who an ability mechanic or a script event affects.
---

Mechanics and some events take a `target:` (or a selector argument) that says **who** is affected.

| Selector | Picks |
|---|---|
| `@self` / `@player` | Whoever is using the ability — the player, or the boss for a mob ability. |
| `@target` | The ability's target: the entity hit (`ON_HIT`), aimed at (`RIGHT_CLICK` with `target-range`), clicked (`ON_INTERACT_ENTITY`), the projectile hit (`on-hit`), or the nearest player (mob abilities with `target-range`). Empty if there's none. |
| `@enemies_in_radius{r=N}` | Every **enemy** within N blocks. |
| `@allies_in_radius{r=N}` | Every **ally** within N blocks. |
| `@cone{range=N, angle=A}` | Every enemy in front, within N blocks and A degrees of where the user is facing. |

**Enemies and allies depend on who's using it:**

| Used by | Enemies | Allies |
|---|---|---|
| A player (item abilities, enchants) | every non-player mob or animal | players, including the user |
| A mob (boss abilities) | players | other mobs |

If you leave `r`, `range` or `angle` out, the defaults are 5 blocks, 8 blocks and 45°
(`items.target-resolver.defaults` in [config.yml](/docs/config-yml#items)). A blank or unknown
selector means `@target`.

Radius and cone selectors are measured from where the ability happens: the user, or the impact
point inside a projectile's `on-hit`.

## Examples

```yaml
target: "@self"                          # buff yourself
target: "@target"                        # the mob you hit
target: "@enemies_in_radius{r=8}"        # AoE around you
target: "@allies_in_radius{r=10}"        # heal your party
target: "@cone{range=10, angle=30}"      # a narrow breath attack
```

Events that take a selector: `heal`, `damage`, `strike_lightning`, `apply_potion`, `give_coins`,
`take_coins`, `entity`. See [Events](/docs/events).
